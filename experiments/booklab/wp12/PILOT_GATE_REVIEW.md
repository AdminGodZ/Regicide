# What the lighter mining gate can and cannot protect

The adopted12 candidate+12 control gate is a reasonable pilot screen if reported as such. It is not a safety
guarantee, and two full-deck runs do not turn each pin into624 relevant tests. My older48-pair primary gate would
not solve the main missing-context problem either.

Concrete harmful case: a pin discovered on one ballot/colour also occurs by transposition on another ballot,
or only after an uncommon opponent book reply. On its tested Cake path it wins often; all12 KingsRow/GuiNN
screening games from the nominating ballot avoid the key. On the other reachable context it loses with probability
10%. Two full-deck runs give only **two trials at that affected opening/colour**; they miss the loss81% of the time.
At25% loss risk they still miss it56.25% of the time. The aggregate624 games per opponent is not the denominator
for that branch. The known w30/w31 position already demonstrates cross-ballot transposition and was reached in a
KingsRow final game. Several retained edits can also create a path into one another that no individual screen saw.

Even if the same affected context were tested12 times plus twice in the whole book, a10% failure probability is
missed in0.9^14=22.9% of campaigns. Twelve loss-free trials have an independent-trial95% upper loss-rate bound22.1%.
Those calculations illustrate coverage risk, not estimates of an actual candidate's risk.

The win gate also admits weak evidence of improvement:4 candidate wins versus1 control win meets it. With12
matched pairs, that can be3 positive non-tied signs and none negative (one-sided sign probability1/8), or4 positive
and1 negative (tail6/32). These are not strong confirmation before selection among up to300 discoveries. A candidate
can also receive unexposed wins after the opponent leaves the line; those wins do not show that the edit helped.

Cheap safeguards compatible with the lighter pilot:

- Retain the lighter gate as an operational screen; require that the qualifying wins actually consulted the changed
  key, but keep all assigned games in WDL and score comparisons.
- Preserve every observed reaching ballot/colour context when deduplicating candidates. Screen known transposed
  contexts against the other engines as well as the nominating ballot.
- In stage4 report per-key eligible exposure, wins/losses and contexts. Give extra repeats to keys with little or
  no effective coverage; a pin with zero exposure is untested, not safe.
- Keep fresh unchanged-r11 controls for the claimed improvement. Without them, new wins can include ordinary
  opponent variation that would also occur under r11.
- If a stage4 loss removes a candidate, those runs have become tuning. Record all failed versions. Either freeze
  the final combination for another untouched confirmation block or call the final two clean runs a selected
  acceptance screen, not independent validation.

This does not require restoring the full48-pair gate everywhere. It requires matching the claim to the evidence,
and focusing the extra games on actual exposure and rare branches rather than counting irrelevant deck games.
